*   Email address (e.g., if you sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth)

#### Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

### Specific Features and Services Used by the App

To provide a fully functioning experience, Streaker utilizes several specific technologies and third-party services which may collect, process, or store your data:

**1. Data Storage (Local & Cloud Firestore)**
Your habits, streaks, and account information may be stored locally on your device (using SharedPreferences) to provide offline capabilities, and securely synced to our cloud database (Firebase / Cloud Firestore) if you have an active account. This ensures you do not lose your progress if you change devices.

**2. Authentication (Firebase Auth & Google Sign-In)**
We use Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In to allow you to securely log in to the App. These services may collect your email, name, and profile photo for the sole purpose of identifying your account and securing your data.

**3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)**
We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data and diagnostic information to help us understand how users interact with the app, identify crashes, and improve the overall user experience. This data does not personally identify you.

**4. Push Notifications**
With your explicit permission, the Application may send local or push notifications to your Device. These notifications are used to send you reminders about your habits, streaks, and updates. You can manage or disable these notifications at any time through your Device's settings.

**5. AI Coaching Bot (Bot Service)**
Streaker includes a "Daily Coach Check" or Bot Service feature. If you interact with this feature, minimal relevant habit data may be processed by external Artificial Intelligence services to generate personalized coaching, advice, or feedback. We do not use this data for marketing or sell it to third parties.
